About LUNA SEN Insurance

LUNA SEN Insurance, operated by LUNA SEN Group Ltd, supports learners aged 16+ with SEN and disabilities.

We act as a specialized gateway, ensuring you can access robust professional insurance solutions provided by Westminster Insurance without unnecessary friction.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is LUNA SEN? +
LUNA SEN Insurance provides specialist insurance coverage gateways for SEN and disabled learners aged 16+ in the UK.
How do I get a quote? +
Choose your learner status using the pink or blue buttons above to be redirected to the secure Westminster Insurance portal.
Types of insurance? +
We specialise in insurance for qualifications, certifications, and vocational or professional training courses.
Who is eligible? +
Our insurance is available for SEN learners, disabled learners, and EHCP holders aged 16+.
What is an EHCP? +
An EHCP describes a person's special educational needs. While helpful, it's not always required for our products.
How to contact? +
You can reach us via the email support link. We assist with any questions regarding quotations or requirements.
